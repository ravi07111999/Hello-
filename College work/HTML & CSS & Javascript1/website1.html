What is a Balanced Diet?

Introduction Key Components Benefits

Introduction

A balanced diet provides your body with the nutrients it needs to function correctly.

Key Components

Consume:

  • Fresh fruits and vegetables
  • Whole grains
  • Lean proteins
  • Healthy fats
  • Low-fat dairy

Benefits

Eating balanced diet maintains or improves health and boosts energy.

Healthy Weight Loss Tips

Strategies Reminder

Effective Strategies

Healthy weight loss needs diet and lifestyle changes:

  • Eat more vegetables & fruits
  • Choose whole grains
  • Include protein
  • Limit sugars and saturated fats
  • Practice portion control
  • Stay active and get sleep
  • Stay hydrated

Reminder

Slow and steady weight loss is sustainable.

The Importance of Hydration

BenefitsHow Much Water?Tips

Why Hydration Matters

Water regulates temperature, digestion, lubricates joints, transports nutrients, flushes waste, improves skin and supports weight management.

Water Intake

About 8 glasses (2 liters) per day — varies by activity and climate.

Tips:

  • Carry a bottle
  • Drink before meals
  • Choose water over sugary drinks
  • Eat water-rich foods
